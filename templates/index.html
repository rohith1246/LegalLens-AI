Problem with Original Clause

The original clause establishes unilateral, uncapped liability and unfair termination penalties that violate standard commercial norms.

Recommended Replacement Legal Wording 
7. BALANCED LIMITATION OF LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION
(a) Mutual Indemnification: Each party shall defend and indemnify the other party from direct damages arising out of third-party claims resulting from gross negligence or intentional infringement.
(b) Mutual Liability Cap: TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW, NEITHER PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY SHALL EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID IN THE PRIOR TWELVE (12) MONTHS.
(c) Waiver of Consequential Damages: Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages.
